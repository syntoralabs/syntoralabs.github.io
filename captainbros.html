Captain BrosCrimson Run

Take cover, slip past patrols, and switch between two brothers in a 2D tactical action game for Android.

Developed by Syntora Labs.

Switch between Rex and Blaze

Use Rex’s rifle to engage enemies at range. Bring in Blaze and his rockets to clear armored obstacles and groups of enemies.

Fight through the campaign

Dodge incoming fire, use cover, and take down guards quietly. Rescue captives, recover intel, and replay missions to earn more stars.

Train, survive, and upgrade

Practice in the training range, take on waves in Survival, or play the Daily Op. Spend earned Supplies on upgrades in the Armory.

Core gameplay is available offline. Ads require an internet connection.
