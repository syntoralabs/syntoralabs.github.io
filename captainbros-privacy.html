Privacy policy

This policy covers the Android game Captain Bros: Crimson Run, developed by Syntora Labs.

Game progress and settings

You can play without creating an account. The game stores mission progress, scores, upgrades, Supplies, achievements, and settings in app storage on your device. The game uses this data locally to restore progress and preferences.

Advertising data

The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between campaign missions. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the following data for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch times.
  • Device or account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, where available.

Google encrypts SDK data in transit and describes how it handles data in its policies. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures

Ad privacy choices

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to check whether a privacy message is required. When available, go to Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change your choices. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android Settings.

Retention and deletion

The game keeps your local save and settings so you can resume playing. Use Settings → Reset progress to reset active progress while keeping your preferences. To remove local app data, including settings and save backups, clear the game’s storage in Android Settings. Google manages data already processed by its SDK under its own policy.
