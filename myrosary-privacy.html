My Rosary – Pray Daily · Syntora Labs

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 31, 2026
Android package: com.syntoralabs.myrosary

1. About this policy

Syntora Labs (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides My Rosary – Pray Daily (“the app”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact us. Contact: support.syntoralabs@gmail.com.

You can use the app without creating an account. Core prayer features work offline. Advertising and some device-provided speech services may use an internet connection.

2. Prayer information stored on your device

The app stores your prayer progress, selected mysteries, completed-session history and calendar, private intentions, bookmarks, saved reflections, reminder times, and preferences in local application storage. This information supports session recovery, prayer tracking, and your chosen settings.

We do not operate an account system or cloud synchronization service for this information. The app does not send your private intentions, prayer text, chosen mysteries, or prayer history to Syntora Labs servers or include them in advertising requests. We do not sell your prayer content or use it to target ads.

The app does not include a separate Syntora Labs analytics or crash-reporting service. Advertising providers may process their own technical and interaction data as described below.

3. Google AdMob advertising and consent

The Home screen may display banner advertisements through Google AdMob. The app requests non-personalized ads. This does not mean that the advertising service processes no data.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share the following with Google and relevant advertising or service partners to deliver and measure ads, diagnose performance, and prevent fraud:

  • IP address, which can indicate an approximate location;
  • App and advertising interactions, such as launches, ad views, and taps;
  • Technical information about the device, app, and SDK, including diagnostics and performance;
  • SDK-supported device or account identifiers, where available and permitted.

The current Android app removes the Android advertising-ID permission. Other advertising-related information may still be processed. The app does not request precise device location.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to obtain applicable privacy choices and checks whether ads may be requested before initializing advertising. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry, you can reopen it under Settings → Privacy & about → Ad privacy choices. Availability depends on your region and the consent service. Non-personalized advertising may still require consent for certain storage or processing.

Google’s handling and retention of advertising data is explained in its Privacy Policy and information about apps that use Google services. Those services may process information in countries outside your country of residence.

4. Optional spoken prayers

If you enable spoken prayers, the app sends the prayer words to your installed Android text-to-speech engine to generate audio. An installed engine may process text locally or through its provider’s online services, depending on the voice and your device settings. Its provider’s privacy policy applies to that processing.

The app does not record your voice or request microphone permission. Private intention entries are not included in the narration text sent by the app. You can turn spoken prayers off or choose an available offline voice through your device’s speech settings.

5. Reminders and device access

Prayer reminders are optional. If enabled, the app requests notification permission and uses your chosen times and device time zone to schedule local notifications. It may reschedule reminders after the device restarts. Syntora Labs does not send remote push notifications or receive your reminder schedule.

Internet access supports advertising, consent services, and any online services used by your speech engine. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or shared photo library. The screen-awake option keeps the display on during prayer without collecting personal information.

6. Sharing and support messages

When you choose to share a reflection, the app opens Android’s sharing interface. The content you choose to share is passed to the app or recipient you select, whose privacy practices then apply.

If you email support, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments or information you choose to send. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, and handle related requests. Our email provider processes these messages as part of delivering and storing email. Please avoid including sensitive prayer intentions or information about other people unless necessary.

7. Retention and deletion

Local prayer records and preferences remain on your device until removed. Archiving an intention does not erase it. To remove all locally stored app information, use Android Settings → Apps → My Rosary → Storage → Clear storage/data, or uninstall the app. Device menu names may vary.

We cannot remotely retrieve or delete prayer information stored only on your device. Information already shared with another app or recipient is outside the app’s local storage and must be managed with that recipient or service.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to handle your request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of information you have sent us by emailing support. Data processed independently by Google or your speech provider is subject to that provider’s retention and deletion practices.

8. Security and your choices

Local records use the app’s private device storage and are subject to Android’s application protections. Keep your device secure and protect access to it. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts collected data in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

You can manage notifications, speech, reminders, and available advertising privacy choices through the app and Android settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information. Contact us about information we hold; contact the relevant provider for information it handles independently.

9. Children’s privacy

The app does not require children to create accounts or submit personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to Syntora Labs through a support message, contact us so we can review and address it, including deletion where appropriate. Device-provided services and advertising providers have their own privacy controls and practices.

10. Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The current version and its effective date will appear on this page. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.

For privacy questions or requests, email support.syntoralabs@gmail.com.
