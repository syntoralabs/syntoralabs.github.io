The art of noticing

A thousand little discoveries.

Wander through cozy homes, colorful gardens, forest clearings and tropical beaches. There’s something waiting to be found in every world.

01 / EXPLORE

A change of scenery

Travel across 20 worlds, with 50 levels in each. Discover five to eight differences in every puzzle.

02 / UNWIND

Take your time

Play without a countdown. Your progress saves automatically, so you can return to your discoveries whenever you like.

03 / LOOK CLOSER

Every detail matters

Pinch to zoom and move both pictures together. Clear discovery markers help you keep track of what you’ve found.

Easy to begin

Two pictures.
A fresh perspective.

A short, skippable practice puzzle helps you get started. Replay the guide from Settings anytime.

  1. Choose an available level and compare the two scenes.
  2. Tap a detail that’s different in either picture.
  3. Zoom in and explore the smaller details.
  4. Find every difference to finish and continue your journey.

Make yourself comfortable

Adjust music, sound effects and vibration. Choose reduced motion or larger discovery markers in accessibility settings.

Play in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Hindi or Arabic. Follow your phone’s language or choose one in Settings.

Ready when you are

Bundled puzzles work offline and no account is required. An internet connection is needed to load ads.

Hints may offer an optional rewarded ad; if an ad is unavailable, the hint is provided without one. The game also includes occasional ads between levels and banner ads on level selection.
