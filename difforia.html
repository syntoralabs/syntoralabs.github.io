Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026
App: Difforia · Android package: com.syntoralabs.difforia

Syntora Labs (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides Difforia, a spot-the-difference puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact us.

Difforia saves game progress and settings on your device. The app includes Google AdMob advertising and Google's consent tools, which process information as explained below. No account is required to play.

1. Game progress and device storage

Difforia stores information such as completed levels, discovered differences, scores, stars, coin balance, unfinished puzzles, tutorial completion, language and sound or accessibility preferences in local app storage. We do not operate a game-account service or upload your save database to our own servers.

Android or your device provider may include app data in device backups, depending on your device settings. These platform backups are separate from an account or cloud-save service operated by us.

2. Advertising and information processed by Google

Difforia uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for optional rewarded hint ads, interstitial ads between levels, and banner ads on level selection. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available and permitted.
  • Ad and product interactions, such as app launches, taps, video views and impressions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app or SDK performance and technical errors.
  • Privacy selections and consent-related information managed through Google's User Messaging Platform.

This information is used to deliver and measure advertising, support advertising analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain service reliability. Ad personalization and processing depend on your region, privacy choices, device settings and the advertising configuration. Non-personalized ads may still process information for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

3. Your advertising choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request privacy choices where required. When available, open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change those choices. Ad requests are gated by the consent tool's permission to request ads.

You can also review advertising-ID and ad-privacy controls in Android settings; available options vary by Android version and device. Choosing not to receive personalized ads does not necessarily remove ads. You can choose whether to watch a rewarded hint ad.

4. Support messages

If you email support.syntoralabs@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the contents and attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate reported issues and maintain relevant support records. Our email provider processes these messages to provide email services. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other information that is unnecessary for your request.

5. Other collection and sharing

Difforia does not require an account and does not provide in-app purchases in the current version. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location. We have not enabled a separate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration; advertising SDK diagnostics described above still apply.

Information may be processed by the advertising and email service providers described in this policy. We may also disclose information we hold when necessary to meet a legal obligation, protect rights or investigate abuse. Providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable privacy terms and safeguards.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game information remains in app storage until removed. You can delete it using Android Settings → Apps → Difforia → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This resets local progress. Device backups may remain separately under your backup provider's settings and retention rules.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, maintain relevant records or meet applicable legal obligations. You may email us to request deletion of support information we hold, subject to any required retention. Google's retention and deletion practices are described in its privacy policy; clearing app storage does not itself delete information already processed by advertising providers.

7. Children and parents

Difforia does not ask players to provide their name, age or date of birth. Advertising services may process device information as described above. Parents and guardians can contact us with questions about a child's information or to request deletion of information they believe was provided to us. We will review such requests and take appropriate action under applicable requirements.

8. Security and privacy rights

We use reasonable measures to protect information we control. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google's Mobile Ads SDK encrypts its data in transit using HTTPS.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us to exercise rights concerning information we hold. For information controlled by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google. You may also contact your local data-protection authority where applicable.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and update the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request new consent.
