Difforia

Two Worlds. Hidden Differences.

Find your moment of wonder

Slow down. Look closer. Discover the extraordinary hiding in the everyday.

Difforia is a relaxing spot-the-difference puzzle game. Compare two illustrated scenes and tap the details that have changed. Explore 1,000 puzzles across 20 worlds, from cozy homes and colorful gardens to underwater kingdoms and fantasy settings.

How to play

  1. Choose an available level.
  2. Compare the two pictures carefully.
  3. Tap a difference in either picture to mark it.
  4. Pinch to zoom and pan both scenes together.
  5. Find every difference to complete the level and continue.

A short, skippable practice puzzle introduces tapping, zooming and hints. Replay it from Settings whenever you need a refresher.

A thousand little discoveries

  • 1,000 puzzles across 20 illustrated worlds.
  • 50 levels in every world.
  • Five to eight differences per puzzle.
  • Untimed gameplay so you can explore at your own pace.
  • Automatic progress saving.
  • Clear markers for discovered differences.

A little help when you need it

Use a hint to highlight a remaining difference. Hints may offer an optional rewarded ad. If an ad is unavailable, the hint is provided without an ad.

Make yourself comfortable

Adjust music, sound effects and vibration in Settings. Accessibility options include reduced motion and larger discovery markers.

Play in your language

Difforia supports English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Hindi and Arabic. Follow your phone’s language or choose your preferred language in Settings.

Enjoy puzzles offline

All bundled puzzles can be played offline, and no account is required. An internet connection is needed to load ads.

The game includes optional rewarded hint ads, occasional ads between levels and banner ads on level selection.
