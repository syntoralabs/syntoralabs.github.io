Juzu: Japa & Mala Counter

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

In short: Juzu does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Your practice is stored only on your device.

Who we are

Juzu is made by Syntoralabs (“we”). This policy explains how the Juzu app for Android (and, in future, iOS) handles information.

Information we collect

None. Juzu does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. We do not receive your practice data, your mantras, your settings or any device identifiers, and we have no server that could store them.

Juzu contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software.

What stays on your device

To work, Juzu saves the following in its private app storage on your phone:

Counting progressThe current round, completed malas and lifetime totals.
Practice historyRepetitions and malas per day, shown in History.
Your mantrasAny custom mantras you type.
PreferencesTheme, bead type, mala size, sound, haptics, keep-awake, eyes-closed and reminder settings.

This information never leaves your device through Juzu. You can delete it at any time by clearing Juzu’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android’s backup, Android may include Juzu’s app data in your device backup, so your practice can be restored on a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under your account, according to your backup settings and Google’s Privacy Policy. We never have access to it.

Permissions

VibrationFor the gentle haptic with each bead. You can turn haptics off in Settings.
NotificationsOnly for the optional daily reminder. Juzu asks for this permission only when you turn the reminder on.
Run at startupSo a daily reminder you set is restored after your phone restarts.

In eyes-closed mode, Juzu can count presses of the volume buttons while the app is open on screen. The presses are counted as repetitions and nothing else is recorded. Volume buttons work normally everywhere else.

Downloading Juzu

When you install Juzu from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download under its own privacy policy. We receive only the aggregated, anonymous statistics Google Play provides to all developers, such as install counts.

Children

Juzu is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Juzu handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version ever needs to collect data, we will say so here and in the app before it does.
