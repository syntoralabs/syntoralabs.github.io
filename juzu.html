Made for practice

Everything a mala needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

No menus to get through, no streaks to keep. The counting is the product.

Tap or pull a bead

Tap anywhere, or swipe to pull the bead along the cord. A gentle haptic marks each one, and one gesture is always one repetition.

Seven beautiful beads

Tulsi, Rudraksha, Sandalwood, Sphatik, Lotus Seed, Black Onyx and Multicolor, each with its own texture and light.

Any mantra, any language

Devanagari, Tamil, English or your own words. Or count in silence, with no mantra at all.

Eyes-closed mode

A dark, quiet screen that counts a tap anywhere, or a press of the volume buttons on Android.

Rounds your way

108, 54, 27, any number you choose, or unlimited. A finished mala is marked with a soft light, then the next round begins.

A quiet record

Today, this week and a bead calendar for the month. Undo any bead. No scores, no leaderboards.

Three themes

Choose the room you pray in.

Theme and beads are separate choices, so Modern Zen with Rudraksha, or Premium Minimal with Tulsi, are both yours to pick.

Modern Zen

Warm ivory, soft natural light and sage accents. Clean and serene.

Traditional

Cream and saffron with delicate gold ornament. Warm and familiar.

Premium Minimal

Near-black with restrained gold. Elegant and focused.

The small things

Quiet, careful, reliable.

  • A completed mala is marked by a soft band of light and a different, gentle pulse. No confetti.
  • Undo takes back exactly one bead, even right after a mala is completed.
  • Progress is saved with every bead and survives restarts.
  • Daily totals stay correct across midnight.
  • Optional soft sound, quarter milestones, keep-screen-awake and a gentle daily reminder.
  • Works with screen readers, large text and reduced motion.

Private by design

Your practice stays with you.

Juzu has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not even ask for internet access. Everything you count is stored on your phone and nowhere else.
