1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Syntora Labs develops and provides Death Curve. In this policy, “we,” “us” and “our” refer to Syntora Labs. This policy applies to the Android app with package name com.syntoralabs.deathcurve and communications you send us about the game.

The current game has no account registration, developer-operated cloud-save service, player chat or social profile system. This policy distinguishes information used locally by the game from information processed by advertising and support providers.

2. Game data and device features

We use the following information to provide the game:

  • Local saves: completed trips and routes, scores, best times, stars, country completion, virtual coins, passenger totals, coach selections and unlocks, capacity upgrades, and ad-reward claim records. These support progression and help prevent duplicate rewards.
  • Your choices: the bus name you enter, graphics and frame-rate settings, sound and music levels, steering preferences, route weather, day/night preferences and onboarding choices. A bus name is a label within the game; you do not need to enter your real name.
  • Phone region: the game reads a SIM country code when available, otherwise the device’s regional setting, to choose a default left- or right-hand cockpit. This is used locally. This feature does not read your phone number, contacts, messages or precise location.
  • Motion sensors: when tilt steering is enabled, gravity or accelerometer readings are used locally to steer the bus. You can disable tilt steering in Settings.
  • Technical device information: the game uses information such as screen size, graphics capabilities and whether a device is an emulator to render correctly, adapt performance and select test advertising on emulators.

Saved game records are stored in the app’s local storage. Active driving and crash-recovery state may be held only in memory and can be lost if Android terminates the app. We do not operate a server that receives your bus name, local save records or steering-sensor readings, and the game does not intentionally pass those values to AdMob.

Android or your device provider may back up and restore app data according to your device and account settings. Such platform backups are separate from a Syntora Labs cloud-save service.

3. Advertising and Google services

Death Curve uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). The game offers optional rewarded ads for virtual rewards or continuing after a crash, and may show an interstitial ad between completed trips. Rewarded benefits require the advertising service to confirm that a reward was earned.

When these services initialize, request or show ads, or manage privacy choices, Google and advertising partners involved in delivering and measuring ads may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it. This is different from GPS or other precise location access.
  • Device and account-related identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID and other identifiers supported by Google’s SDK and your device settings.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, video views, ad impressions and reward-related events.
  • Diagnostics and technical information, such as app/SDK performance, launch times, errors and device characteristics.
  • Privacy choices and consent status needed to apply the advertising choices available in your region.

These services use information for ad delivery, advertising personalization where permitted, measurement, analytics, diagnostics, security and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the SDK, your region, privacy choices and device settings. Ads are not necessarily personalized, and selecting a less-personalized option does not mean all advertising-related processing stops.

Read more in Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. The game does not include a separate Syntora Labs player-analytics or cloud crash-reporting service.

4. When you contact support

If you email support.syntoralabs@gmail.com, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot problems and handle privacy requests.

Our email provider processes those communications to deliver and store email. Support messages are separate from locally saved game data and are not automatically deleted when you clear or uninstall the game.

5. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by Google and advertising partners for the purposes described above, and by the providers that deliver our support email. Information sent through support may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to a valid legal request, or protect users, our services or legal rights.

Providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy notices describe their processing locations and applicable safeguards. Local game saves are not intentionally uploaded by the game to a Syntora Labs backend.

6. Your choices and deleting information

  • Advertising privacy: where required, the app presents a Google privacy message. When available, open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review or change the choices offered by Google’s consent system.
  • Advertising ID: use your Android device’s privacy or advertising settings to reset or delete the advertising ID or manage advertising personalization. Exact controls vary by Android version.
  • Local game data: open Android Settings → Apps → Death Curve → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall the game. This removes local saves, bus names and settings from that installation. Menu wording may vary. Manage any Android/device backups separately, because a backup may retain or restore app data.
  • Bus name and controls: change the bus name in Garage and adjust tilt steering, cockpit position, audio and graphics in Settings.
  • Support information: email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to our support address, subject to applicable legal obligations.

We cannot remotely access or erase saves stored only on your device. Clearing the game does not by itself delete information previously processed by Google or other providers. For provider-held information, use the relevant provider’s privacy controls or contact process. You can also contact us for help identifying the appropriate process.

You may skip optional rewarded ads and restart a failed trip for free. Core driving works offline; ad-supported benefits need connectivity and an available ad.

7. Retention and security

Local saves remain until they are cleared, removed or replaced, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. We retain support communications for as long as needed to address your request, resolve related issues and meet applicable legal obligations. Advertising providers retain information according to their own policies, including Google’s retention policy.

We use the protections provided by Android app storage and our service providers and limit our use of support information to the purposes described here. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can provide an absolute security guarantee.

8. Privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, request a copy, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or raise a concern with a data-protection authority. Where advertising controls offer consent withdrawal or an opt-out, you can use those controls as described above.

To make a request concerning information handled by Syntora Labs, contact support.syntoralabs@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify and respond to your request. We can act on information we hold; requests about independently held Google advertising data may need to be made through Google’s privacy tools.

9. Children’s privacy

Death Curve is a general-audience game and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly solicit children’s personal information through support. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
