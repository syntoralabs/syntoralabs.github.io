1. Who we are

Chain Chaos (“the app”, “the game”) is a physics puzzle game developed and published by Syntora Labs (“we”, “us”, “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why, who it is shared with and the choices you have. It applies only to the Chain Chaos app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. The game has no registration, login, chat, social features, user-generated content or payment system. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, photos or phone.

The app requests only two Android permissions, both required to load ads:

  • Internet access (INTERNET) – to request and display ads.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) – to check whether a connection is available before requesting an ad.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the app may also use the Android Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID). All gameplay works fully offline; ads simply do not appear without a connection.

3. Data stored on your device

To save your game, the app keeps a small local database on your device containing:

  • Levels completed, stars earned and best scores.
  • Your settings (music and sound volume, haptics, reduced motion, reduced effects, graphics quality).
  • The placement data for your most recent successful solution.

This data never leaves your device through the app and is not sent to Syntora Labs. The app also keeps a short, temporary in-memory log of gameplay events (for example “level started”) to support its own features; it is discarded when the app closes and is never uploaded.

If you have Android’s backup feature turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Chain Chaos is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). The app shows:

  • Rewarded ads – only when you choose to watch one to unlock a puzzle hint.
  • Interstitial ads – occasionally between levels (never during play).

When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process the following from your device:

Syntora Labs does not receive it in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports such as the number of ad impressions. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised (based on your interests) or non-personalised (based on general context).

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. How data is used

  • App functionality – saving progress and settings locally so you can continue where you left off.
  • Advertising – displaying ads that fund the free game, and granting a hint only after a rewarded ad is completed.
  • Fraud prevention, security and compliance – performed by Google on ad traffic.

We do not use your data for profiling by Syntora Labs, automated decision-making with legal effects, or any purpose unrelated to the game.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. The only third party that receives data from the app is:

  • Google (AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform) – an advertising service provider, receiving the data listed in Section 4 to serve, measure and protect ads and to record your consent choices. Google may share data with its own advertising partners as described in its policies.

We may disclose information if required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others. Because we do not hold personal information on our own systems, such requests are unlikely to concern data held by us.

7. Consent and your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): on first launch the app may show Google’s consent message, letting you accept or refuse personalised ads and related data processing. Where applicable, you can change your choice at any time in the app via Settings → Help & Privacy → Privacy options.
  • US state privacy rights: where applicable, Google’s consent tools allow you to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Hints are optional: rewarded ads are only shown if you tap Hint and confirm. Skipping them never blocks gameplay.
  • Play offline: if your device has no network connection, no ads are requested and no data is sent.

8. Retention and deletion

Game data stored on your device remains until you delete it. You can remove all of it at any time by clearing the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Chain Chaos → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s own retention policies (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

Because Syntora Labs does not store personal data about you, there is no account or server-side record for us to delete. If you have questions about deletion, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

Game data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access on a standard Android device. Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit risk by not collecting personal information ourselves.

10. Children

Chain Chaos is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed primarily at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps. Where ads are shown to users identified as children, they are requested in accordance with Google’s families and child-directed ad policies.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, to object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Since we do not hold personal data that identifies you, most requests regarding ad data are best directed to Google, but we will gladly help—contact us below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.
