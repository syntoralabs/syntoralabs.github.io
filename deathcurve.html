Know your road

A glimpse of the route.
Then the real challenge.

New Classic and World Tour journeys open with a 3D route map and flyover. See the climb ahead, then tap Start Driving when you are ready.

  • Learn mobile tilt steering in an optional hands-on mountain lesson.
  • Pick up passengers, manage speed and brake before sharp bends.
  • Earn in-game fares and stars, and unlock more routes and coaches.
  • Personalize your bus name and adjust controls, camera views and graphics.
Play your way

Mountains to master.

Explore World Tour chapters across India, Nepal, Norway, Switzerland, Peru, Japan and Romania, or take on Classic challenges.

Route data is adapted for gameplay. Scenery is stylized and procedural; Death Curve is not a navigation service or an exact recreation of real roads.

Advertising & privacy

Your game. Your choices.

Core driving works offline. The app includes Google AdMob ads; advertising features require an internet connection. Optional rewarded ads can provide supported in-game bonuses or a continue after a crash.

Game progress and preferences are saved on your device. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, interactions and diagnostics as described in our Privacy Policy. Where available, advertising privacy choices can be managed in the app’s Settings.
