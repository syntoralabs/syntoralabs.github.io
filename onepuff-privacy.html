Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026
Android package: com.syntoralabs.onepuff

This policy explains how Onepuff, provided by Syntora Labs, handles information when you use our Android app. Onepuff lets you log cigarettes and view your smoking history. No account is required.

1. Your smoking history

Onepuff stores cigarette logs, their dates and times, and information needed to calculate your totals in a local SQLite database on your device. App preferences, advertising milestone information, and privacy choices may also be stored locally.

We do not operate a cloud service for your smoking history. The app does not upload your smoking logs to our servers or provide those logs to Google as advertising targeting data.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Onepuff displays banner and interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob. Although your smoking history stays local, advertising services can collect and share information independently of that history.

Depending on privacy choices and device settings, Google's advertising SDK can process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. These services use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, maintain their services, and prevent fraud.

The current app requests non-personalized advertisements. Non-personalized ads can still involve identifiers and data processing for measurement, security, and other advertising functions.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

3. Privacy choices

Onepuff uses Google's User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy choices. Where a privacy message is required and available, the app presents it before requesting ads. When Google requires access to privacy options, an in-app privacy-options button lets you review your choices.

You can also manage advertising identifiers and available ad privacy controls through your Android device settings. Available controls vary by device and Android version.

4. Backup and restore

You can export your smoking history to a JSON backup file in a location you choose and restore a compatible file later. Exported files contain your logs and are not encrypted by Onepuff. Anyone with access to a backup file may be able to read it.

Choosing a cloud storage provider through your device's file picker may transfer the file to that provider under its own privacy policy. Android or your device's backup services may also back up app data depending on your settings. Onepuff does not provide its own automatic cloud sync.

5. Retention and deletion

Local history remains on your device until you delete individual logs, replace history through restore, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. To remove all local Onepuff data, use Android Settings, open Onepuff's app information, and clear its storage. Menu names vary by device.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not remove exported backup files or copies held by device or cloud backup services. Delete those separately and review your backup settings to prevent restoration of unwanted data.

Information processed by Google or other providers follows their own retention and deletion policies. We cannot directly access or delete your device's local logs.

6. Security and permissions

The app stores its local database in app-private device storage. Backup files are stored wherever you choose. Protect your device and backup files using appropriate security settings.

Onepuff uses internet and network-state access for advertising and related privacy services. Logging and viewing smoking history work offline. The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location.

7. Children's privacy

Onepuff is intended for adults who want to record their smoking habits and is not directed at children. We do not ask users to submit a name, age, email address, or account profile inside the app. If you have a concern about a child's use of Onepuff, contact us.

8. Contacting us

If you contact us, we use the information you choose to provide to respond to your question or request. Avoid sending smoking history or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.
