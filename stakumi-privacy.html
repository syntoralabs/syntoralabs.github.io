About this policy

This policy describes Stakumi (Android package com.syntoralabs.stakumi), a physics stacking game. It covers the app's own storage and the advertising SDKs included in this version. Advertising behavior can depend on consent, regional rules, audience settings, and whether test ads or live ads are enabled.

Game progress stored on your device

Stakumi stores level progress, scores, earned coins, records, completed goals, onboarding status, and sound/accessibility preferences in app-local storage. There is no Stakumi account, cloud save, or Syntora Labs gameplay server. The app's local analytics implementation does not send gameplay analytics to Syntora Labs. Local data remains until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to the device's own backup and restore behavior. To remove it, clear Stakumi's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app.

Advertising and third-party processing

Stakumi includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When ads can be requested, these services may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, device/app information, ad interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Google processes that information under its own terms and privacy policies. The app gates ad requests on UMP's canRequestAds result and currently requests non-personalized, G-rated advertising with conservative child/under-age treatment. Those settings do not mean that Google processes no information. Ads can be unavailable without preventing you from playing. Rewarded ads are optional. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Google advertising information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads Google Mobile Ads data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Privacy choices

Where applicable and available, use Settings > Advertising privacy choices to open Google's privacy-options form. You can also manage advertising identifiers and ad privacy settings in Android settings. UMP message availability depends on the publisher's AdMob configuration. Contact support.syntoralabs@gmail.com for questions or privacy requests. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal data.

Permissions and offline features

The app uses internet access for advertising and consent services. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or shared-media access. The how-to-play video and game assets are bundled with the app and work offline. Result sharing copies text to the clipboard only when you choose that action.

Children and audience settings

Stakumi has no account registration or in-app chat. Its advertising requests use conservative child/under-age settings in this version. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy concerns. The publisher must declare the actual target audience accurately in Play Console and configure applicable advertising and consent settings before distribution.

Data sent to support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request. We do not receive your device-local game progress unless you provide it. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The effective date will be updated. Questions: support.syntoralabs@gmail.com.
