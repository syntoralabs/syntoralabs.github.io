Stone Up Privacy Policy
Last updated: 11 October 2026

Stone Up is provided by Syntora Labs. This policy explains how the Android app (com.syntoralabs.stoneup) handles information.

Game data on your device
The app stores campaign progress, control, sound and haptic preferences, advertising cooldowns, and any previously earned coin balance on your device. These records are not sent to a game server. Stone Up does not create user accounts or ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location. Android device backup may copy local app data according to your device settings.

Advertising and third parties
Stone Up uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show banner ads in chapter browsing and occasional interstitial ads between levels. Rewarded coin ads are disabled in the current campaign.
Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising requests are transmitted using HTTPS. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Information about the SDK's data handling: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Privacy choices
Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents advertising privacy choices before ads are requested. You can revisit applicable choices through Settings > Ad privacy. This entry appears when Google's consent service requires a privacy-options entry. You can also manage advertising identifiers and privacy settings in Android settings. The game remains playable when ads are unavailable or consent prevents advertising.

Retention and deletion
Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it; device backups may be retained separately under your device's backup settings. Clearing storage resets progress and preferences. Data processed by Google follows Google's retention and deletion policies. See Google's privacy policy for details and controls.

Children's privacy
Stone Up is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact Syntora Labs at support@syntoralabs.com. Google advertising privacy choices and age-of-consent requirements may also apply to teenagers in some regions.

Contact and updates
For privacy questions, email Syntora Labs at support@syntoralabs.com. This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change.
