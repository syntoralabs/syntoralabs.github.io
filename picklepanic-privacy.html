1. Who we are

Pickle Panic (“the Game”) is provided by Syntora Labs (“we”, “our”, or “us”). This policy applies to the Android version of Pickle Panic.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game may save your preferences locally, including selected character, gameplay settings, audio options, and other settings needed to remember your choices. The Game does not require an account to play.

If an age-screen feature is enabled, the Game may store an age category locally to determine appropriate advertising and privacy treatment. The exact year of birth need not be retained for this purpose.

3. Advertising and third-party services

If advertising is enabled in the version you use, Pickle Panic uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display ads. Google's services may automatically process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or app identifiers, subject to the SDK's configuration, your settings, and applicable law. We do not control the content of individual ads.

Advertising features may be absent or use test ads in pre-release builds. If the installed build does not request ads, advertising-related collection described in this section does not occur through an active ad request; SDK behavior must still be evaluated separately.

Learn more about Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

4. Children and families

Pickle Panic is designed for a broad audience, including children. Where advertising is enabled, we aim to apply age-appropriate protections, including restricted ad content and child-directed treatment where required. We do not intend to use interest-based advertising for children.

Users who do not provide an age category should be treated conservatively for advertising purposes. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the Game may present a consent or privacy-choice message before making eligible ad requests. Available choices depend on your location and the advertising configuration. If a “Privacy options” control is available in Settings, you can use it to review applicable choices. You may also contact us using the address below.

6. How information is used

  • To operate the Game and remember local preferences.
  • To display and manage advertising when enabled.
  • To support security, debugging, and reliability through the services actually present in the release build.
  • To meet applicable legal and privacy obligations.

We do not sell a player database or require players to create profiles.

7. Sharing and retention

Local preferences generally remain on your device until changed, cleared, or removed with app data; operating-system backup behavior may vary. When advertising is enabled, Google may process data according to its own retention and privacy practices. We do not operate an account backend for Pickle Panic.

8. Your controls

You can change gameplay preferences in Settings and may clear locally stored app data or uninstall the Game. For information processed by Google, consult Google's privacy controls. To ask a question or raise a privacy request, contact us below.

9. Security

We take reasonable care in selecting and configuring services used by the Game. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Our privacy disclosures will be updated if the Game's data practices materially change.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Changes will appear on this page with an updated date. If a change requires further notice or consent, we will provide it where applicable.
