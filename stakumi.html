Easy to learn. Satisfying to master.

Every good tower starts
with one careful drop.

Each level brings a new balancing puzzle. Reach the height or stack goal, then keep your creation standing long enough to complete the challenge.

  1. 01 / AIM

    Find your position.

    Drag horizontally to line up your next piece. Take a moment to read the shape of your tower.

  2. 02 / ROTATE & DROP

    Make it fit.

    Rotate in 15-degree steps, release your object, and let the physics settle into place.

  3. 03 / HOLD

    Keep your balance.

    Reach the target and hold steady. If your tower falls, restart and try a fresh approach.
