A little wisdom.
Every single day.

Make the Thirukkural part of your everyday life. Dhinam Oru Kural brings you a daily Kural, with Tamil and English meanings to help you read, reflect, and return to the verses that matter to you.

Timeless words, a daily habit

The Thirukkural, composed by Thiruvalluvar, explores virtue, wealth, and love through short, thoughtful couplets. Our app makes these ideas accessible through a calm reading experience you can carry with you.

Start with today’s Kural or explore the collection at your own pace. The verses are stored on your device, so reading does not depend on an internet connection.

Made for everyday reading

A Kural each day

Build a regular reading habit with a daily verse and its meaning.

Tamil and English

Read in Tamil, English, or both, with translations and explanations.

Read offline

Access the locally stored Kural collection even when you are away from the internet.

Find your next verse

Search by Kural number or words, explore chapters, and browse virtue, wealth, and love.

Save and share

Keep favourite Kurals for later and share verses through your device’s sharing menu.

Make it your own

Choose light, dark, or sepia themes and set a daily reminder time that suits your routine.

Begin with a moment of reflection

Open the app, read today’s Kural, and spend a moment with its meaning. Save a verse you want to revisit, share it with someone, or explore another chapter.

No account required

Your favourites and reading preferences are stored locally on your device. The app includes Google AdMob advertising; see our privacy policy for details about advertising and data processing.
